Efficiency Tips & Financial Return

• Retiring a secondary refrigerator saves on average 800–1,200 kWh/yr (approx ₹6,400–₹9,600 / year).
• Converting all lighting to 100% LED drops continuous baseload draw.
• Homes with electric resistance heating in cold climates show up to 3× electrical consumption compared to gas/hydronic systems.

Methodological Note & Survey Limitation

Non-Panel Cross-Sectional Structure: The EIA RECS surveys independent samples every 4–6 years; it is not a longitudinal panel tracking identical households over time. Consequently, survey_year captures structural shifts (e.g. federal efficiency standards, LED adoption) rather than continuous time series trends. Holding out the full 2020 cycle guarantees zero data leakage across survey waves.

Preprocessing & Missing Codes Handling

• RECS Missing Codes: Special response codes -2 (Not Applicable), -8 (Don't Know), -9 (Refused), and -99 were mapped to NaN and handled with feature-specific median imputations.
• Weight Adjustments: Survey replicate weights (e.g., BRRWT1–96, NWEIGHT1–60) were dropped from feature matrices to avoid overfitting on survey sampling design artifacts.
• Outlier Trimming: Electricity consumption targets were bounded between 1st and 99th percentiles to eliminate erroneous extremes.
